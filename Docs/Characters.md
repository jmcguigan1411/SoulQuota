# Soul Quota - character bible (character pass, Nov 25 2026)

Every reaper should look, move, play and sound like nobody else on the roster. This file is the record of who each one is and
what makes them unlike the other twenty. The numbers live in the data: `Reapers.csv` (body, gait, trait, bio, tip, difficulty,
playstyle, barks), `Kits.csv` (passive, E, R incl. the new Mod columns), `Upgrades.csv` (signature weapons), `FxRecipes.csv`
(generated by `blender_work/fx2`). Previews: `/mnt/user-data/outputs/chars/` (lineups, per-character sheets) and
`/mnt/user-data/outputs/fx_v3/` (effects).

## Audit (before this pass)

- **One body plan.** All 21 stood 1.5-2.2 m on the same skeleton with the same leg length, head size, arm length and
  shoulder width; ArtScale only ranged 0.79-1.18. In silhouette they differed by hats and props only. From the chase camera
  (behind, 18 deg down) Tickwell / Beak / Pallbearers / Ectoplasm / Collector read as the same dark robed column, and
  Bonesmith / Digby / Vexa as the same green-brown block.
- **Gait data unused.** Every `Gait*` column was neutral (0/1); the per-character feel was baked into the clips only.
  Floating monsters (Grim, Widow, Actuary) stood on the floor.
- **Kits overlapped.** Five E dashes with knockback (Grim, Monk, Femur, Neckless, Vexa), three E blinks (Tallow, Sweep,
  Tickwell), two E fans of projectiles (Mort, Intern), two near-identical shield bashes (Bonesmith, Solace: Circle Shield|Knockback),
  two slow+knockback cones (Widow, Digby); R: two crew-speed stampedes (Mort Rush Hour, Neckless Wild Ride, plus Femur's haste
  howl), two map-wide heal/revives (Beak, Solace), three pull zones (Widow, Intern, Digby), four novas.
- **Same feel on the move.** No reaper had a movement rule of its own; only base speed differed.
- **Character select** showed a fixed-height model on the generic `Idle` clip (not the reaper's own idle), no bio, no
  difficulty, no playstyle.

## What changed (systems)

- **Body proportions** (`Reapers.csv` BodyLegs/Torso/Chest/Head/Arms/Hands, `FSQBodyProfile`): applied in
  `USQAnimInstance` (`ApplyBodyShape`) as uniform bone scales after the pose, legs compensated at the root so feet stay
  on the ground. Heights now 1.14-2.94 m (ArtScale 0.60-1.55). Collision radius per reaper (`HitRadius`, 28-54 cm).
- **Gait personalities** filled in for all 21 (hover, hunch, bounce, sway, limp, stride, arm swing, lean, head bob).
- **Showroom / lobby** use the same native anim instance as a run: own idle, gait, body, and own size
  (`ShowroomScalePow`); fidgets are the reaper's own flourish / bank / signature takes, cheers its own victory take.
- **Ability mods** (`Kits.csv` E_/R_ Mod, ModValue, ModTime): Charges, Recast, Stacks, Trap, Detonate
  (`USQAbilityComponent::TickEMods / DoRecast / QueueModFollowUps`). HUD shows charges / stacks on the E slot.
- **Movement traits** (`Reapers.csv` Trait*): one condition x one effect per reaper, all 21 pairs unique. Conditions:
  Always, AfterDash, AfterCast, Moving, Still, Carrying, LowHealth, NearAlly, Alone, Crowd, AllyDowned, NearLantern. Effects:
  Speed, Fortify, Damage, Phase (walks through the horde), Shove (pushes the horde aside).
- **Barks**: short lines over the head (level up, low health, revived, R cast, boss, victory); text now, lines below are
  the script for future voice.
- **Lobby kit panel**: bio, playstyle, difficulty pips, movement trait row; your pick shows its play tip.
