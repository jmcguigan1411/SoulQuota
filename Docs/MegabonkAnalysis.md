## 5. Maps, stages, tiers, run structure

**Maps (3)** [S12][S27][S17]:

| Map | Unlock | Layout | Gimmick / hazards | Bosses (?) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forest | Start | Hills, valleys, trees, rocks, medieval ruins; cover and vertical routes | Foliage hides enemies; secrets (banana, Monke cage + key, boomboxes, Suspicious Bush statue) | Lil Bark (T1), Chadbark (T2), Bark Vader (T3) (?, megabonk.org only). Minibosses: Chunkham, Stone Golem [S14] |
| Desert | Reach stage 2 of Forest T2 as CL4NK | Flat, open, dunes, ruins; little cover. Tornadoes lift you up | **Sandstorms** cut visibility to almost zero; Skeleton Balls; scorpions; cactus artillery; tumbleweeds (5% Katana/Shotgun) | Lil Anubis / Anubruh / Juge Anubis (?). Minibosses: Sand Golem, Scorpion(ussy); secret Bandit statue |
| Graveyard (Dec 2025) | Beat "Calcium's Dad" in Desert T2 as Calcium (4 graves, then golden statue) | Outdoor graveyard plus an indoor **Crypt** sub-area | 4 coffins, each a local boss that drops a Crypt Key; need all 4 keys to escape. **Light/dark**: stand in lamp light or die in dark waves. Escape timer | Big Bob (lamps lower his defence) -> Scythe; **Spooky Steve** (huge HP) spawns if you fail to escape -> Roberto |

PC Gamer also mentions **indoor lava-floored temples** that demand precise platforming [S20]. "The Floor is Lava" is also a Desert challenge modifier [S16].

**Maps are procedurally generated** and have height and verticality [S1][S3].

**Run structure** [S11][S14][S12]:
- Pick a map and a tier. Tier 1 = 1 stage. Tier 2 = 2 stages (Forest then Desert in early routing (?)). Tier 3 = 3 stages + final boss. Map difficulty multipliers are 0.75x / 0.95x / 1.1x (?) [S14].
- **Stage timer:** 10 minutes. One player guide says 10 / 9 / 8 minutes for T1 / T2 / T3 (?) [S14].
- Timeline (T1 stage): 3:00 miniboss, 4:00 swarm, 7:00 swarm, 8:00 miniboss, **10:00 Final Swarm** [S11].
- **Boss Portal** is at a random spot on the map and can be started any time. Beating the boss vacuums all XP to you and opens the next-stage portal (T2/T3) [S11][S14].
- **Final Swarm:** endless ghosts that get faster. Normal ghost, then chasers after 1 minute, then purple (cannot be executed) at 6 minutes, then red (lethal) at 12 minutes. Silver multiplier up to 8x (was 16x). Reworked in v1.0.12 because world-record runs lasted 5+ hours at ~1.5 FPS [S13][S14][S25].
- **Final boss** (T3): ~3 phases. Shields at 75/50/25% are disabled by standing at 3 pylons. Teleports you to an arena, **disables all non-starter weapons**, ~10-minute timer, ~9.1M HP (player report (?)) [S14][S12].
- Typical run: ~10 min (T1) to ~30 min (T3); Speedrunner challenge targets are 6:00 / 14:00 / 30:00 [S16].

## 6. Enemies and bosses

**Enemies** [S14] (player guide (?)):
- Forest: exploding goblins, slimes, skeletons (**blue = guaranteed level-up, gold = gold**; both flee), fast runners, flying bees, golems, ogre.
- Desert: "grandma" type, skeletons, cactus groups (artillery warning), red wisps, poison scorpions, rolling mechs / Skeleton Balls.
- Graveyard: undead, coloured ghosts from graves, crypt bosses [S17][S27].
- Elites wear crowns: more XP and a chance of a free chest [S14].
- Final Swarm ghosts (see above).

**Bosses / minibosses (~15 named):** Lil Bark, Chadbark, Bark Vader, Lil Anubis, Anubruh, Juge Anubis (all (?)), Chunkham, Stone Golem, Sand Golem, Scorpionussy, Bush (secret), Bandit (secret), Calcium's Dad (secret), Big Bob, Spooky Steve, plus the final boss [S12][S14][S17][S28]. Tier 3 bosses "weaken your character and nerf your abilities", have shields (no lifesteal) and puzzle elements [S29b].

## 8. Meta progression, quests, difficulty

- **Silver** = meta currency. It comes from challenges, silver pots, the Silver Tome and Final Swarm (multiplier up to 8x). Silver is **scarce** [S3][S14][S13].
- **Quests (240):** "do X" goals such as kill counts, tome levels, secrets and bosses. Completing a quest makes an item buyable with silver [S1][S8][S36].
- **Shop (silver):** +2 weapon slots, +2 tome slots, Refresh / Skip / Banish (up to ~6-7 charges each), Silver gain (+2.5% each, 20 buys), Boombox (music choice), **Toggler** (switch unlocked items on or off, needs ~40-50 unlocks). Most are refundable [S14].
- **Challenges (difficulty modifiers):** about 13 per Forest tier, e.g. Fragile (no hits), Turbo, Smol Boi, AFK, Blind (fog), Sticks and Stones (no items), Weird Controls (reversed), Speedrunner/+, Pacifist, Oh no / Oh shit / What the fuck (1.25x / 1.5x / 2x enemies). The Desert adds Hyper, The Floor is Lava, Swarm, Chonk Mode. Each pays a silver multiplier (1.5-2x) and +1% permanent silver gain on first clear [S16].
- **Difficulty stat:** Greed Shrines, Cursed Tome, Skuleg and Sir Chadwell raise it (more and stronger enemies, more rewards) [S14].
- **Leaderboards** (with cheating problems; global ban list since v1.0.12) [S25][S2].
- **Hats:** 21 hats from achievements (v1.0.64) [S23].
- There is **no** easier or assist mode (players ask for one) [S19].

## 9. Movement

[S18][S14][S3][S30]

- WASD, **jump** (characters have 1.0-2.15s airtime), **extra jumps** (items like Feathers, and some characters), **slide** (Ctrl: builds speed downhill from ~10 to ~35-60), **bunny hop** (slide + jump chains), **air strafing**, Ninja/Monke can change direction mid-air ("catapult").
- **Fall damage exists**. Wall-hugging or sliding down slopes reduces or cancels it. Spaceman ignores it.
- Monke climbs walls; Birdo glides; Desert tornadoes act as launchers.
- **No dedicated dodge or dash**: guides tell you to save a jump as your dodge [S14][S30].
- **No grind rails or jump pads found in any source** (?).
- Movement tech is a big part of the skill ceiling and the speedrun community [S18].

## 10. What players and critics PRAISE

- **Addictive "one more run" loop** - ~37% of review themes [S19]; "addictive" [S3]; PC Gamer 88/100: "brilliant dumb fun" [S20][S21].
- **3D terrain and verticality** - TheGamer praised the "added 3D terrain and verticality" [S3]; PC Gamer liked maps as "messy collections of painful drops, bottlenecks, and rewards tantalisingly out of reach" [S20].
- **Build variety and synergies** (~10%), **tight controls** (~9%), **easy to pick up** (~9%), **soundtrack** (~8%), **value for money** (~8%), **lots of unlockables** (~8%) [S19].
- **Silly humour and characters** (~7%) [S19][S20].
- **Runs on low-end hardware, handles hundreds of enemies** [S19][S20].
- **Exploration rewards**: chests, shrines and salesmen spread across the map [S20].

## 11. What players and critics COMPLAIN about

From the Steam review analysis [S19] unless noted:
1. **Luck/RNG decides runs** (~19%), and the luck stat feels inconsistent.
2. **Poor balance / forced meta builds** (~17% + 4%).
3. **Lack of updates** (~11%) - the solo dev went quiet. Confirmed: no Steam news since Feb 2026 [S2].
4. **Too punishing** late bosses and high tiers (~10%); an insta-kill boss surprise [S20]; **no easier modes** (~3%).
5. **Repetition / too few maps** (~6%); IGN criticised the "lack of changing scenery" with only two maps at review time [S3]; interest fades after 5-10 hours once unlocks are done [S19].
6. **Enemies spawn on top of you** (~4%); crashes and lost runs (~4%); late-game FPS drops [S19].
7. **Needs a wiki** to understand stats and items; poor in-game explanations [S19].
8. **Precise platforming** (lava temples) is "far less fun" [S20].
9. **Meme humour** is too much for some (IGN, GamesHub; ~4%) [S3][S19].
10. **No co-op** - a small but steady request (~2% of reviews) [S19]; widely asked in press FAQs [S24].
11. **Repetitive challenges, difficulty spikes** (GamesHub) [S3].

## 12. Story question

**Does Megabonk have a story? No.** No plot, no lore delivery, no characters with arcs. The review analysis lists "Story: None" [S19], and no critic review mentions one [S20][S3].

**Evidence across the top survivors-likes:**

| Game | Story / framing | Sales evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Vampire Survivors | Almost none. Developer: the game "has no plot", which makes a film hard to picture [S31]. Later added **"Adventures"** as *optional* "self-contained miniature story modes", joking about discovering the trend "called 'having a story'" [S32] | Genre-defining multi-million seller (exact total not verified here) |
| Brotato | No story (play a potato, survive waves) [S35] | **10M+** by 2025 [S35] |
| Megabonk | No story; humour and character silliness carry it [S19] | ~2.7M (estimate) [S22] |
| Halls of Torment | Minimal (?) | 70k in first week [S37] (headline) |
| Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor | Light framing inherited from the Deep Rock Galactic world (dwarves, missions, biomes) | 500k in EA week 1; 1M+ [S36] (headlines) |
| Ball x Pit | **Light framing**: ruined city of Ballbylon, a meteor pit, plus a village-building meta between runs; no deep plot [S33] | 300k in 5 days, 1M by Dec 2025 [S33]; 2M+ reported [S34] (headline) |

**Conclusion:** none of the hits sold on story. Every one sold on loop, builds and personality. What *does* recur among the bigger recent hits is a **strong premise or theme plus a light meta wrapper** (DRG's world, Ball x Pit's pit-and-village, Megabonk's absurd cast, Lethal Company's corporate quota satire). Story-heavy delivery (cutscenes, long dialogue) has no evidence of helping and costs dev time. Players skip text in this genre. Recommendation in ContentPlan.md: **light framing** - keep the Death's Office satire, memos and contract pages, all skippable, and put most of the effort into the loop.
